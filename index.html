About

Elvin is a London & Istanbul based, East 15 trained actor and theatre-maker.

Her most recent work includes the BBC's upcoming series Shy & Lola and Fragments from the East, a feature film that premiered in the official selection of the International Antalya Golden Orange Film Festival and continues to screen at other international festivals.

She wrote and is currently performing in her debut play, Gallows and Children, at The Space Theatre and Camden Fringe, with upcoming shows in London and Europe with Barbar Collective, a company she co-founded dedicated to bold, politically charged theatre. Her work often combines physical theatre, clown and personal storytelling, exploring themes of identity, belonging and memory.
